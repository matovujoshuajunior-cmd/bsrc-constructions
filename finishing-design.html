Finishing & Design FAQ

What finishing packages do you offer?
We offer painting, plastering, molding, full interior fit-out and compound design, tailored to your budget and lifestyle. Every package can be customised.
Do you handle interior designing alongside finishing?
Yes. Our interior designers work hand-in-hand with the finishing team so the final space feels cohesive — furniture, lighting and finishes are considered together.
Do you work on existing buildings or only new builds?
Both. We finish new builds and re-finish or restyle existing homes, offices and commercial spaces.
