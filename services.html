Frequently Asked Questions

After inquiring for your services, are you responsible for material search or am I responsible?
Material supply depends on negotiation between us and the client. If you prefer handling the task, we cooperate strongly with your suppliers. If you hand over the task to our team, we involve our trustworthy suppliers.
Do you have authorised skilled and trained labour for our projects?
Yes, we use registered, approved architects, civil engineers, mechanical engineers, builders and certified craftsmen, each with a minimum national certificate and 6 months of verifiable experience.
How do you handle legal building laws enforcement for all building classes?
We strictly follow and enforce legal building laws. During consultation, we help clients understand how to obtain building permits and avoid penalties, ensuring full compliance.
How far do you operate from your headquarters?
We are highly versatile and can operate in every region of Uganda, as long as the site is accessible and basic human needs are available.
