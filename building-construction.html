Building & Construction FAQ

Do you build outside Kampala?
Yes. We operate in every region of Uganda as long as the site is accessible and basic human needs are available.
Who handles material supply?
Material supply depends on negotiation. You can supply your own materials and we cooperate strongly with your suppliers, or you hand the task to our team and we involve our trustworthy suppliers.
Can you handle building permits?
Yes. We strictly follow and enforce legal building laws and help clients understand how to obtain building permits and avoid penalties during consultation.
