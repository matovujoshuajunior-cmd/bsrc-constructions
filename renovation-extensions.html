Renovation & Extension FAQ

Can I extend my existing building legally?
Yes. All our extension work is legal and non-destructive. We handle the required permits and follow every building regulation for your area.
Do you renovate old buildings?
Yes. We renovate buildings of any age, from colonial-era structures to recent builds. Older buildings sometimes need structural review, which we handle before drafting the plan.
How long does a typical renovation take?
Timeline depends on scope — a single-room renovation may take a few weeks, while full-home or second-storey projects take longer. We give you a realistic schedule in our estimate.
